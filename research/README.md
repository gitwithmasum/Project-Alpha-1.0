# Research Pilot Studies

This directory contains three completed computational pilot-study manuscripts by Masum Billah.

## Manuscripts

1. **Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Monthly Climate Stress Early Warning Across Bangladesh Divisional Cities**
   - NASA POWER daily climate data, 2014-2024
   - Temporal validation on 2022-2024
   - Best ROC-AUC: 0.923; best PR-AUC: 0.821

2. **Machine Learning for Next Day Extreme Rainfall Early Warning in Bangladesh**
   - Rainfall-based flood-risk proxy
   - Test recall: 78.1%; PR-AUC: 0.348
   - This is not a river-level or inundation forecast

3. **Carbon Aware Machine Learning Model Selection Under Accuracy Constraints**
   - Breast Cancer Wisconsin, Wine, and Digits datasets
   - Digits benchmark: 93.9% estimated training-energy reduction for a 0.31 percentage-point accuracy difference

## Research status

These manuscripts report completed pilot experiments. They have not been peer reviewed or accepted by a journal or conference. Each paper documents its datasets, methods, results, and limitations.
